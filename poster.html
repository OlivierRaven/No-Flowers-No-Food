No Flowers = No Food
Let Nature Grow — Plant native • Mow less (smarter) • Stop spraying • Keep corners wild

Act for Aotearoa’s insects

  • Grow native (hebe/koromiko, mānuka & kānuka, harakeke, olearia, pittosporum).
  • Mow less (2–3×/year), never all at once; avoid Oct–Jan.
  • Skip sprays: hand-weed, mulch, dense planting.
  • Leave structure: leaf litter, twigs, logs, brush piles.
  • Think in layers: tussocks → shrubs → trees (kōwhai, mānuka, ribbonwood).
  • Join local efforts: council, iwi, Forest & Bird, community groups.
