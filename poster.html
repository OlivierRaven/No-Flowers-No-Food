No Flowers = No Food
Plant native • Mow less (smarter) • Stop spraying • Keep corners wild

Act for Aotearoa’s insects

  • Grow native: koromiko, mānuka/kānuka, harakeke, olearia, pittosporum.
  • Mow less: 2–3×/year; never all at once; avoid Oct–Jan.
  • Skip sprays: hand-weed, mulch, plant densely.
  • Leave structure: leaf litter, twigs, logs, brush piles.
  • Think in layers: tussocks → shrubs → trees.
  • Join locals: council, iwi, Forest & Bird, community groups.
