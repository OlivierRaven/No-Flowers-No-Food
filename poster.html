No Flowers = No Food
Plant native • Mow less (smarter) • Stop spraying • Keep corners wild

What you can do

Grow Native Plants Hebe (koromiko), Mānuka, Olearia
Mow Grass Less 2–3×/year, leave ~30% long, avoid Oct–Jan
Stop Spraying Weed by hand, use mulch, or plant densely
Make Shelter Spaces Leave leaf litter, twigs, and logs for nesting

The world is in a biodiversity crisis.

Insects are disappearing, and without them, we risk losing our food.

Even small patches of flowers in gardens, schools, or streets make a big difference.
